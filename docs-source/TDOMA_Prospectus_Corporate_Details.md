## Background
Established on 30 December 2019 G.C. by ten founding shareholders with the objective of
formalizing trading activities within Merkato. Incorporated as TDOMA S.C. (Tokkummaa
Daldaltoota Oromoo Markaatoo — "Unified Oromo Merchants in Merkato") as a share company
under Commercial Code Articles 304–509, with an initial subscribed capital of ETB 10 million,
contributed by the founding shareholders through ordinary shares with a par value of
ETB 1,000 each.

On 26 November 2024 G.C., TDOMA held its first Board Meeting in five years and adopted
strategic resolutions, including revisions to the Company's MoA and issuance of new strategic
directives, laying the groundwork for the Gabaa Merkato Commercial Center (GMCC) project.

As of the date of the document, the Company has expanded to 91 shareholders and increased
its paid-up capital to ETB 21.7 million. Its principal planned activity is the development of the
GMCC, a mixed-use commercial building to be constructed in Addis Ababa within the Addis
Ketema District. The Company has not yet secured legal land ownership for the project; it has
completed a feasibility study and prepared a strategic document (January 2026) outlining the
project's core activities and development direction.
